FAQ

Common questions

Do you work with clients outside the UK and Sweden?

Yes. I work remotely with clients worldwide, communicating by email and video call and fitting around your time zone where possible.

Can you take over an existing WordPress or PHP website?

Absolutely. I regularly pick up existing sites and codebases — I'll start with a review to understand how things are built, flag any risks, and then fix, improve or extend them.

Can you upgrade a site running on an old version of PHP?

Yes. I upgrade legacy applications and WordPress sites to supported PHP versions, fixing incompatible code and outdated dependencies along the way so your site stays secure and fast.

Should I use WordPress or a custom PHP application?

WordPress is ideal for content-led sites, blogs and many online shops where you want to edit pages easily. A custom PHP or Laravel application suits bespoke business logic, portals and tools. I'll recommend whichever genuinely fits your needs and budget.

How much does a project cost?

It depends on scope. Well-defined projects get a fixed price up front; ongoing or evolving work is charged at a day or hourly rate. Get in touch with a few details and I'll give you a clear quote.

Do you offer ongoing support and maintenance?

Yes. I offer monthly retainers covering updates, backups, security monitoring and small improvements, as well as ad-hoc support when you need it.
